WedRoll Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Ryota Masuda (the "Operator") handles information received through WedRoll (the app and the camera page, the "Service").

1. Information we receive

WhoseInformationPurpose
HostEvent name, date, time zone, photos per guest, reveal timeTo create the invitation page and album and to schedule the reveal and deletion
HostPurchase records (transaction ID and a random ID the app creates per device)To verify purchases and apply them to the event
GuestNickname, photos taken, time takenTo show the photos in the album to the Host and other Guests
GuestReports (which photo was reported)To hide reported photos until the Host reviews them
EveryoneIP addressTo stop floods of requests. Counted in server memory only and never stored in the database
HostCrash reports (device model, OS version, error details)To fix bugs. No names or photos are included

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or location. Location and other metadata in photos are removed when the server receives them. We do not track you for advertising.

2. People who appear in photos

Photos taken by Guests include other people. Photos can be seen only by the Host and the Guests who joined through the invitation QR code; opening the album requires the key stored on the device when joining, or the Host's key. Anyone who appears in a photo, invited or not, can ask us to delete it by email. We review and delete the photo on request.

3. Retention

  • Photos, nicknames and reports: deleted automatically 30 days after the reveal, or immediately when the Host deletes the event.
  • Events that were never purchased: deleted 30 days after the planned reveal time.
  • Purchase records: to stop a purchase being used twice, we keep only the link between the transaction ID and the event ID after the event is deleted. The event name is erased.

4. Service providers

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare R2): photo storage
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: in-app purchase management (random device ID and purchase records)
  • Apple Inc. / Google LLC: payment for in-app purchases
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry): crash reports from the app

These providers may store data outside Japan, for example in the United States.

5. Information kept on your device

The Host app keeps the event management key in the device's secure storage. A Guest's browser keeps the join key, nickname and any photos not yet sent in its own storage. Photos are removed from the browser once sent.

6. Sharing

Except where required by law, we do not share information with anyone other than the providers above.

7. Requests

To request access to, correction of or deletion of your information or photos you appear in, email m.ryota1222@gmail.com.

Operator: Ryota Masuda
