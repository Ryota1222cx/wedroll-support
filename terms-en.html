These terms set out the conditions for using WedRoll (the app and the guest camera page, the "Service") provided by Ryota Masuda (the "Operator"). They apply both to people who create an event (the "Host") and to people who join through an invitation QR code ("Guests").

1. The Service

The Host creates an event and shares an invitation QR code. Guests take photos on the camera page. At the time the Host sets, the photos are revealed in an album that only the Host and the Guests of that event can see. Photos are deleted automatically 30 days after the reveal.

2. Fees

The app and the camera page are free to use. To let Guests join and to reveal the album, the Host buys a guest tier for each event at the price shown in the app. Guests are never charged.

3. Photos and people in them

  • The Operator uses photos only to provide the Service (storage, thumbnails and delivery), never for advertising or training data.

The Operator may delete photos that break these rules without notice.
