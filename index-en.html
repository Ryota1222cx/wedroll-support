WedRoll Support

WedRoll hands every wedding guest a single-use camera. The couple creates the event in the iPhone or Android app and shares an invitation QR code. Guests scan it and take photos in their browser, with no app to install. At a time the couple chooses, all the photos come together in one album.

Contact

For bugs, feedback or a request to remove a photo, email m.ryota1222@gmail.com. For removal requests, include the event name and roughly when the photo was taken.

FAQ

  • Do guests need the app? No. Scanning the QR code opens the camera page in the browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android). If the camera does not work inside an app such as LINE, choose "Open in browser" from the menu.
  • How many photos can each guest take? The couple decides per event. The default is 27 photos per guest.
  • When can we see the photos? At the reveal time the couple sets (by default 9 a.m. the day after the wedding). Until then nobody, including the couple, can see other people's photos.
  • When are photos deleted? 30 days after the reveal, automatically. Save the photos you want to keep before then. The couple can save all photos at once in the app.
  • A photo I do not want shown: Guests can tap "Report" on a photo. Reported photos are hidden from other guests until the couple reviews them, and the couple can hide any photo. To have a photo deleted, email us.
  • Purchases: One purchase per event. There are no recurring charges. If you need more guests, you can buy a larger tier for the same event.
